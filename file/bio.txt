Dr. Zhiqiang Lin is a Distinguished Professor of Engineering and Director of the Institute for Cybersecurity and Digital Trust (ICDT) at The Ohio State University. His research spans systems and software security, including automated binary analysis, vulnerability discovery, malware analysis, software hardening, virtualization, and trusted execution environments. More recently, his work has expanded to AI security, with a focus on agentic AI, including vulnerabilities in AI-generated code, securing autonomous AI agents and their interactions with tools and environments, and building trustworthy foundations for AI-driven software and cyber operations. He is an IEEE Fellow, an ACM Distinguished Member, and a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award, AFOSR Young Investigator Award, Distinguished Paper Award from IEEE S&P, Distinguished Artifact Award from NDSS, and Distinguished Reviewer Award from CCS. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University.
